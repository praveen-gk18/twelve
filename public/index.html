CredShield

Two dedicated portals for issuing and verifying digital credentials

A blockchain-inspired credential system built around two workflows: the issuer portal for institutions and the verifier portal for employers, universities, and recruiters.

Issuer portal

For institutions to onboard issuers, issue digital credentials, generate PDFs and QR codes, anchor proofs, and revoke certificates when needed.

  • Approve or suspend issuers
  • Issue a credential for a student or employee
  • Generate QR + PDF certificate
  • View anchor hash and revoke credentials
Go to issuer portal
🔎

Verifier portal

For HR teams, universities, and recruiters to verify a credential instantly using the credential ID or the QR link generated from the issuer portal.

  • Instant trust score and validation status
  • Check issuer approval, hash match, signature validity, and live status
  • Preview credential metadata, chain anchor, QR, and PDF
  • Simulate tampering to show why forged edits fail
Go to verifier portal
