How the credential lifecycle works

The product separates issuance and verification into two focused experiences while keeping the data flow straightforward.

1. Institution approves issuer
The trusted registry ensures only authorized organizations can create credentials.
2. Credential is created and signed
Payload data is normalized, hashed, and signed before a proof record is anchored.
3. QR and PDF are generated
The holder receives a shareable certificate with a direct link into the verifier portal.
4. Verifier checks live trust
Status, issuer approval, signature validity, and hash integrity determine authenticity.
