Verification logic

What the portal checks before declaring a credential authentic

Issuer approved

Confirms the institution exists in the trusted issuer registry.

Hash matched

Ensures the visible certificate data maps exactly to the stored proof.

Signature valid

Validates the cryptographic signature created by the issuing organization.

Status active

Rejects certificates that were revoked or have passed their validity date.
